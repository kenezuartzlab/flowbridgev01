FlowBridge has officially launched on BOT Chain Mainnet.

FlowBridge is a non-custodial Web3 toolkit bringing Trade, Bridge, MultiSend, Staking, Rewards and Flow AI into a guided ecosystem experience. Its production network is BOT Mainnet, chain ID 677.

## Why BOT Chain

FlowBridge builds around BOT Chain's ecosystem of liquidity venues, native assets and cross-chain infrastructure. The aim is useful participation: understandable actions, transparent contracts and genuine activity, not artificial transactions or engagement.

## Live product capabilities

- **Trade:** Live route discovery and quotes through FlowBridge routers, with review, price-impact visibility and explicit approvals. Supported routes distinguish atomic execution from staged transactions; availability depends on current liquidity and simulation.
- **Bridge:** Guided transfers using external official BOT Bridge integrations. Source confirmation is not destination completion; destination receipt must be verified. Unsupported directions remain unavailable.
- **MultiSend:** Prepare and review distribution, consolidation and advanced transfer workflows. Each wallet confirms its own required transactions; Activity groups the session while preserving receipts.
- **Staking:** Flexible, 30D, 90D, 180D and 365D are publicly available on BOT Mainnet. Terms, availability and rewards are read from contracts. Flexible and 30D have real-mainnet maturity evidence; 90D, 180D and 365D have simulation evidence and live positions still awaiting actual maturity. Their real-maturity verification is not complete. The price oracle remains disabled.
- **Rewards:** Verified activity supports server-derived FLOW progression and claims. A displayed balance is not a promise of claimability: the 1,000 FLOW minimum, funding and applicable claim conditions must be satisfied.
- **Flow AI:** Guided ecosystem discovery and action preparation. AI does not sign or spend for the user; live availability and wallet review govern execution.

## Wallet confirmation, contracts and transparency

Connecting a wallet does not authorize spending. Ownership binding is a separate explicit signature. Token approvals and execution are separately reviewed and confirmed in the wallet. Success is based on confirmed receipts and canonical Activity, not on a submitted hash alone. A cancelled signature is not a completed transaction.

Read the current [Contracts & Security documentation](https://flowbridge.space/docs), [BOT Chain ecosystem page](https://flowbridge.space/bot-chain) and public whitepaper before using a product. FlowBridge-owned contracts and external BDEX, CaSwap and official BOT Bridge integrations are labeled separately; historical or quarantined contracts are not active deployments. Recognition does not establish a partnership or program approval.
