# AutoTrade rules
- AutoTrade lives in src/lib/autotrade/ (pure engine/executor/worker helpers + server quotes/worker) behind /api/admin/autotrade, Ops → AutoTrade and the scheduled /api/public/hooks/autotrade-worker; orders are paper-only while AUTOTRADE_LIVE_ENABLED=false, signer stays NOT_CONFIGURED, registry changes are previews only, and live calls must pass assertExecutionAllowed. Why: no real funds move without a dedicated trading account, independent RPC and explicit signer approval.
- The worker runs only under the fenced DB lease (autotrade_acquire_lease/heartbeat) and fails closed on RPC divergence. Why: one writer per order and no decisions on stale chain data.
- Canary preparation is read-only; managed live effects enter through processManagedLiveEffect with a source kill switch and durable IO, never the negative-chain paper store. Why: a cap approval or paper receipt cannot authorize a mainnet signature.
