# AutoTrade rules
- AutoTrade lives in src/lib/autotrade/ (pure engine/executor/worker helpers + server quotes/worker) behind /api/admin/autotrade, Ops → AutoTrade and the scheduled /api/public/hooks/autotrade-worker; orders are paper-only while AUTOTRADE_LIVE_ENABLED=false, signer stays NOT_CONFIGURED, registry changes are previews only, and live calls must pass assertExecutionAllowed. Why: no real funds move without a dedicated trading account, independent RPC and explicit signer approval.
- The worker runs only under the fenced DB lease (autotrade_acquire_lease/heartbeat) and fails closed on RPC divergence. Why: one writer per order and no decisions on stale chain data.
- Canary preparation is read-only; managed live effects enter through processManagedLiveEffect with a source kill switch and durable IO, never the negative-chain paper store. Why: a cap approval or paper receipt cannot authorize a mainnet signature.
- Canary review arithmetic lives in canaryTicket.ts; generated local gas footprints are revalued server-side, expire with the quote and never substitute for exact dedicated-account simulation. Why: stale fixtures and review tickets must not authorize real orders or unlimited gas.
- Managed dispatch uses separate exact-intent, nonce and effect tables through autotrade_managed_effect, with attempt-level CAS, lease fencing and total gas reservations; worker calls dispatch behind the source switch. Why: concurrent restart paths must not sign twice or reuse paper nonces.
- Paper forms and APIs share strict fixed-point validation in paperForm.ts; bounded clientRequest calls never retry writes automatically, and reviewed request UUIDs persist through ambiguous results with same-actor/parameter checks against the order primary key. Why: mobile auth stalls, decimal rounding and repeated taps must not silently change or duplicate a paper order.
- AutoTrade permissions are decided only in paperPermissions.ts: any verified app_admins Ops role with its bound wallet may create and manage its own paper orders (server-forced mode=paper, per-account quota, audit); emergency, canary, infrastructure and registry actions stay super-admin only. Why: paper simulation needs no financial privilege, but policy and live-path controls must not widen.
